faq-q: What does "take" mean in Basic English?
faq-a: To take means to get a thing into your hands or to remove it from where it was. It is word 10 of the 850 and the first verb of loss in the book.
faq-q: Why is "It takes" the scariest line so far?

faq-q: What is the difference between "take" and "get"?
faq-a: Get is neutral receiving; take is reaching out and removing. You get a gift, you take a thing from someone. English keeps the difference alive in fixed phrases: get a letter, take a chance.
faq-q: What forms does "take" take?
faq-a: take, takes, taking, and two irregular forms: took and taken — it took time, it was taken. The chapter keeps its drumbeat on the simple form, and this answer gives you the whole family at once.
