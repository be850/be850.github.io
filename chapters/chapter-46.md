faq-q: What does "all" mean in Basic English?
faq-a: All says the says of them as one: not one of them, all of them. You say all when the says go as one keep, no one of them left out: all the says, all the keeps, all the comings. It is word 46 of the 850 &mdash; the next word in the line that began with "about". faq-q: How does "all" work with the other words?
faq-a: All works with the words that put things in lines and groups. With of: "all of the says" &mdash; of says what the all holds: not one say, all the says of them. With till: "all of it is with you till the one after comes" &mdash; till says how long the all stays with you. With may: "you may take all of it or one of it" &mdash; may says the take is open; all says how much it holds. With from: "one came from all" &mdash; from says where the one starts: it starts in the all. With the: "the all of the says" &mdash; the points at one all; all says how many: every one of them.
faq-q: What is the difference between "all" and "a"/"the"?
faq-a: A takes one from the rest and does not say which one; the takes the one before you and says which one; all takes them as one and says how many. You say "a say" when the say is one of the says; you say "the say" when the say is the one before you; you say "all the says" when the says go as one keep, no one of them out. A is the take of one; the is the seeing of the one; all is the keep of them as one.
