The keep of all was before me; I had every one of the says of it in one keep. Any had let one of them go; the keep went with the one it let go. I put every say of the keep before me, one by one; every say came before me; every say was one after the one before it.

They came to the keep with me; the keep had every say of the says in it. I took one of them; I saw the say of it; after it I took the one after it; every take was one take; every take was of the keep, one by one.

The says of the keep went before me one by one; every say came; every say went; the keep kept them as one. I saw every one of them come; I saw every one of them go; the keep was the keep after every one of them. The going of one went with the going of them all; every say had the say of it; the says of the keep went on.

Every one of the says went by me; the keep was before me as one keep, with every say of it in me. The keep of me had every say of the keep of them; every one of them was in me, one by one. The one after every was coming to the keep; I saw it come from before me; the keep of them was with me till it came.
