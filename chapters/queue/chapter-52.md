The other says: the keep said no to the take of them; they went from the keep; one of them came after the no. The one after the no is the other. I saw the two of them: the one went from the keep, the other came to the keep. The give of the keep is at no; the take of the other came to no, as the take of them came to no.

The other says: much of the say of him went with them; the little of the say is with me. The keep of all keeps the two of them: the little with me, the much with them. No said no to the take of them; no says no to the take of the other; the other came to the keep; the keep of all said no to the other, as it said no to them.

The other says: I am of the keep of all; the one with me is the other of us. The keep of all has one of us; the other of us came to the keep of all. The keep gave the little of it to me; the much of it went with them; the other came for the much of it; the keep said no. The keep was as it was; the other came after the no; the keep keeps the little of it with me.

<i>one of us was the say of him; the other of us was me; they came for the one of us; they took the say of him; they got no little of him; the keep said no to them; I am the other; the keep keeps me with the little of him.</i>
