faq-q: What does "where" mean in Basic English?
faq-a: Where is the word that says the place of a go &mdash; the from of it and the to of it. A say goes, and the where says where the say went: it went from a from, and it came to a to. The where takes no say over a say and no say under a say &mdash; the who gave the ones of the say, the how gave the go of it, the when gave the before and the after of it, and the where gives the from and the to of the go. In this book where is word 70 of the 850 &mdash; the word that came after when.
faq-q: How does "where" work with the other words?
faq-a: With how: how says the go of a say, and where says the from and the to of that go. With when: when says the before and the after of the go, and where says where the go went &mdash; the place of the from and the place of the to. With because: because puts the from under the say, and where says where the go went on that from. With the keep: the keep keeps the says and the goes of them, and the where of every go comes from the one that sees the says come and go.
faq-q: What is the difference between "where" and "there"?
faq-a: There says a place as a thing you point at; where says the place of a go as a part of the say. "I went there" points at the place and stops at it; "where did the say go?" makes the place of the go a part of the say itself. There stays still, and where goes with the go &mdash; the where is of the go, not of the thing.
faq-q: What word comes after "where"?
faq-a: The next word is "why" &mdash; word 71 of the 850. Where says the from and the to of a go; why will put its say on the from of a say itself &mdash; the from under the from, the thing a say stands on. Where gave the says their place in the go; why will give them the thing under the go.

In this chapter the hero looks for the where of the go: the from of the say of him, the to of the go of me &mdash; and every where he finds comes back to one place. It was me.

The where says: a say goes, and the where says the from and the to of the go of it. The who came with the say and gave the ones of it; the how put the go on the say; the when put the before and the after on the go. The where takes no say over a say and no say under a say: it goes with the go of the say and says where the go went — from a from, and to a to. i may see a say go, and i may put where on the go of it — where did the say come from, and where did it go? The where gives no who and no how; the say of the where is the from and the to of the go.

The where takes no go from the say and gives no go to the say: the go is of the say — the how said it — and the where is the from and the to of the go of it.
