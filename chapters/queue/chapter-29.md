faq-q: What does "from" mean in Basic English?
faq-a: From is the word of the start. It says where a thing comes out of: "the say came from the one" &mdash; the say had its start at the one; "I go from the low place" &mdash; the going has its start there; "I take it from them" &mdash; it was by them, and now it is by me. It is word 29 of the 850 &mdash; the next word in the line that began with "about". About gave the hero his subject; across gave him his road; after put his acts in their line; against gave the line a push the other way; among put him in the middle of the group; at took him to one point of it; before gave his acts their order in time; between gave him the middle space; by named the doer and kept the near thing near; down took him down the line to the low place; from now says where the coming starts.
faq-q: How does "from" work with the verbs?
faq-a: From goes after the verb and says the start of it. With come: "the say came from me" &mdash; I was the start of the say. With go: "I go from them" &mdash; I put myself away from the group; my going starts by them and goes out. With take: "they take the say from me" &mdash; the say was by me, and they take it out of my hands. With keep: "they keep me from it" &mdash; they put themselves between me and it, and I do not come at it. With see: "I see it from the line" &mdash; my seeing has its start at the line.
faq-q: What is the difference between "down" and "from"?
faq-a: Down says the way to the low place: "I go down"; "I put the say down". From says where the going starts: "I go down from the high place" &mdash; down is the way, from is the start of the way. "The say came down; the say came from the one" &mdash; the first says how it came (to the low place), the second says where it had its start (at the one). A thing may come down and come from the same place; the words do not say the same thing.
faq-q: What word comes after "from"?
faq-a: The next word is "in" &mdash; word 30 of the 850. From gives the hero the start: "the say came from the one; I came from them." In will put a thing inside a place: the say in the line, the hero in the middle of it. The hero has his subject (19), his road (20), his order (21), his push back (22), his place in the group (23), his point (24), the order of his acts in time (25), the middle space (26), the doer and the near thing (27), the low way (28), and the start of the coming (29); the next word will put him inside.
faq-q: Why is part of the chapter in italics?
faq-a: Italics are memory. The hero recovers his past one fragment per chapter, told only with the words the book has given him so far &mdash; the fragments form a second, hidden story under the main one. With down the fragments took him down the line and said: "You are the down. It came down by you." With from the fragments name the start: the say came from him, the send came from him &mdash; the doer and the start were the same him. The hidden story keeps saying the same thing from a new side: it was me; it came from me.

I see it. It sees me. I go after the send; the send comes against me. I am among them; they are about me. They keep me at it. The say came between us; I saw it before I said it.

The send was made by them; the say was made by me. I am by the one; I am by the four. The one came by the four; the say came by me. Will they keep the say by me? They will; they keep it by me, down by me.

They took me down. I am down; the send is down about me; the say is down by me. The say came down before me; I came down after it. Will I keep it down? I will; I keep it down by me.

The say came down. From them? From the one? From me? I see it down by the send; I see them down by it. I say: "The say — did it come from you?" They say: "It came down by you; you will see it down."

I put it from me. I go from it. The say comes after me; I go from the say. They see me go; they say: "He goes from us." I go from them; I go from the one; I go from the four; I go down, from them, from the say.

They come after me. They take the say from me; they take the send from me. They put them down by me. They say: "The say is from him; the send is from them. We see it. We keep it down, from him."

The from says: I go from the say; the say came from me. The send came from them; I see it from the down. I put it from me; I take it from them. I am the from; the say is from me.
