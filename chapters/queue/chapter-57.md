faq-q: What is the difference between "i" and "me"?
faq-a: I is the one that says; me is the one the say is of. You say i when the me does: i came, i saw, i keep, i give. You say me when the say is about the me: the say of me, the some came to me, the says went through me. i is the start of the say &mdash; the doer, the one the going comes from; me is the rest of the say &mdash; the one the say is of, the one it comes to. In this book the two go together: the me that says is i; the say that is of the me is the say of me &mdash; the say is of me, and the me is in the say; no take goes between them.
