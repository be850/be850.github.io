faq-q: What does "down" mean in Basic English?
faq-a: Down is the word of the low way. It says where a thing goes when it goes to a lower place, or along: "I go down after the say" &mdash; the going goes down the line; "they take me down" &mdash; they lead him to the low place; "I put the say down" &mdash; he sets it in the low place, he writes it. It is word 28 of the 850 &mdash; the next word in the line that began with "about". About gave the hero his subject; across gave him his road; after put his acts in their line; against gave the line a push the other way; among put him in the middle of the group; at took him to one point of it; before gave his acts their order in time; between gave him the middle space; by named the doer and kept the near thing near; down takes him down the line to the low place.
faq-q: How does "down" work with the verbs?
faq-a: Down goes after the verb and says the way of it. With go: "I go down after the say" &mdash; the going is downward, along the line. With come: "The send came down; the say came down after it" &mdash; the things came from the high place to him. With put: "I put the say down" &mdash; he set it in the low place, he wrote it. With take: "They take me down" &mdash; they lead him down. With keep: "They keep me down by them" &mdash; they keep him in the low place, near them.
faq-q: What is the difference between "by" and "down"?
faq-a: By names a doer or puts a thing near a thing: "the say was made by me"; "I am by the send". Down says the way to the low place: "I go down"; "I put the say down". By is the word of the doer and the near thing; down is the word of the low way. A say is made by him and then put down by him.
faq-q: What word comes after "down"?
faq-a: The next word is "from" &mdash; word 29 of the 850. Down takes the hero down the line to the low place: "I put the say down; I put it down at the one". The send came down, the say came down after it &mdash; the next chapter will say where they come from. The hero has his subject (19), his road (20), his order (21), his push back (22), his place in the group (23), his point (24), the order of his acts in time (25), the middle space (26), the doer and the near thing (27), and the low way (28); the next word will give the start of the coming.
faq-q: Why is part of the chapter in italics?
faq-a: Italics are memory. The hero recovers his past one fragment per chapter, told only with the words the book has given him so far &mdash; the fragments form a second, hidden story under the main one. With between the fragments showed what stood in the middle; with by the fragments named the doer. With down the fragments take him down the line: the say came down about him, the four were down about him, they said: "You are the down. It came down by you." The hidden story keeps saying the same thing from a new side: the doer was always him; it was me.
