The say keeps with me. I go with it; it goes with me. The send comes; I see it come. I let it come; I see it as it is. The say goes with me as the going goes; I take it as I take the going.

The as says: I keep the say as the keep keeps the say; I go as the say goes; the say is as the keep. The send comes as the send comes; I see it as it is; I let it go as it goes. One say goes as one keep: the say as the keep, the keep as the say.

<i>They saw me as them; I went as them.
The say was as the keep; I kept it as they kept it.
They said: he goes as we go; he is as we are.
I was as the one; the one was as me.
The say came as they sent it; it was with me as it was with them.
It was me as them.</i>
