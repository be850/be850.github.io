faq-q: What does "of" mean in Basic English?
faq-a: Of says what a thing is part of &mdash; the say of the send, the keep of the say, the end of the going. It is word 41 of the 850 &mdash; the next word in the line that began with "about". About gave the hero his subject; across gave him his road; after put his acts in their line; against gave the line a push the other way; among put him in the middle of the group; at took him to one point of it; before gave his acts their order in time; between gave him the middle space; by named the doer and kept the near thing near; down took him down to the low place; from said where the coming starts; in put him inside; off took a thing away; on put it back; over took it over; through took it through; to took the act to its end; under put the keep under the send; up took the say up from under; with keeps the say with him; as says the being of one in the place of the other; for says who the give is for; of says what the give is part of.
faq-q: How does "of" work with the other words?
faq-a: With give: "the give of the say" &mdash; the give belongs to the say; it is part of it. With keep: "the keep of the say" &mdash; the keep is the say's own keep. With make: "I make the send of the say" &mdash; the send is made out of the say. With come and go: "the coming of the send; the going of the say" &mdash; of names the thing each act is part of. With take: "the take of the say" &mdash; the take belongs to the say, as the keep belongs to the keeper.
faq-q: What is the difference between "of" and "for"?
faq-a: For says who the give serves; of says what the give is part of. "The send was for me" &mdash; I am the one it serves. "The send was of them" &mdash; it came out of them; it is part of them. In the chapter the send was for the hero (for) and it was of the coming (of): first the purpose, then the belonging. A send may be for one and of another.
faq-q: What word comes after "of"?
faq-a: The next word is "till" &mdash; word 42 of the 850. Of says what a thing is part of: the say of the send, the keep of the say, the coming of the send. Till will say how long the act goes on: the say till the coming, the keep till the going. The hero has his subject (19), his road (20), his order (21), his push back (22), his place in the group (23), his point (24), the order of his acts in time (25), the middle space (26), the doer and the near thing (27), the low way (28), the start of the coming (29), the inside (30), the take-away (31), the put-back (32), the over (33), the through (34), the end of the act (35), the keep under (36), the going up (37), the keep with (38), the being as (39), the give for (40), the say of (41); the next word will say how long it goes on.
faq-q: Why is part of the chapter in italics?
faq-a: Italics are memory. The hero recovers his past one fragment per chapter, told only with the words the book has given him so far &mdash; the fragments form a second, hidden story under the main one. With under the fragments said where the say was kept. With with they said the going with. With as they said the being in the place of. With for they said who it was all for. With of they say what it was part of: they made the say of me; the say of the send was of them; it was the me of them. The hidden story keeps saying the same thing from a new side: the say was part of him from the first; it was the me of them.

The say is of me; I am of the say. I came to the keep with the say; the say came with me. The send of the coming was for me; I took it up; I gave it on. I see the going; I see the coming; I put the say of it between them.

The of says: I am of them; they are of me. The say is of the send; the send is of the coming; the coming is of the say. The keep of the say is the keep of me; the keep of me is of the say. Of says of the say: it is of him; it is of her; it is of them.

I give the say of me to you; I give it for them. The give of the say is of the say; the get of it is of you. Of goes with to: the give goes to him; the say of the give is of her. Of goes with for: the send was for me; the send was of them. A give to him may be of her; a say for them may be of me.

The say came from them; it was of them from the coming. I put the say under the four; the four of the say was of the keep. I took the say up from under; the up of it was of the going.

The sends came; they were of the coming. I put them by; I saw them; they were of them. I gave them up; they went of them. Of says it: the say was of me from the coming; the coming was of the say; the say was of me. I was of the say as the say was of me.

The of goes before the doing: I make the send of the say before I make it; I come of the say before I see it. A say may go to him; it may be of her. The say of me came to me; it was of you. I got it at the keep; I gave it up for you. To took the give to him; for says the one the give was for; of says the say of the give.

I keep the say of the coming. The of says: the say is of me; I am of the say; we are of the say as it is of us. I take it up; I give it on. I may keep it for you; I will. The say of me goes as I go; I go as the say of me goes.
