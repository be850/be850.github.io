The say is of me; I am of the say. I came to the keep with the say; the say came with me. The send of the coming was for me; I took it up; I gave it on. I see the going; I see the coming; I put the say of it between them.

The of says: I am of them; they are of me. The say is of the send; the send is of the coming; the coming is of the say. The keep of the say is the keep of me; the keep of me is of the say. Of says of the say: it is of him; it is of her; it is of them.

I give the say of me to you; I give it for them. The give of the say is of the say; the get of it is of you. Of goes with to: the give goes to him; the say of the give is of her. Of goes with for: the send was for me; the send was of them. A give to him may be of her; a say for them may be of me.

The say came from them; it was of them from the coming. I put the say under the four; the four of the say was of the keep. I took the say up from under; the up of it was of the going.

The sends came; they were of the coming. I put them by; I saw them; they were of them. I gave them up; they went of them. Of says it: the say was of me from the coming; the coming was of the say; the say was of me. I was of the say as the say was of me.

The of goes before the doing: I make the send of the say before I make it; I come of the say before I see it. A say may go to him; it may be of her. The say of me came to me; it was of you. I got it at the keep; I gave it up for you. To took the give to him; for says the one the give was for; of says the say of the give.

I keep the say of the coming. The of says: the say is of me; I am of the say; we are of the say as it is of us. I take it up; I give it on. I may keep it for you; I will. The say of me goes as I go; I go as the say of me goes.

<i>They made the say of me.
The say of the send was of them; I was of the say.
They kept the keep of me; I kept the say of them.
They said: he is of the say; the say is of him.
I came of them; they came of me.
The say of me was of the coming.
It was the me of them.</i>
