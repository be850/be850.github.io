Every say has a who. i say the say of me — the who of it is me: the say goes with the one that says it. he said the say of him — the who of it was him. The say of you will go to you — the who of it will be you. The say went from i to he, from he to you; with who the say gets the one it is of — the say goes on, the who goes with it.

The who says: every say comes from one. The keep of all keeps the says of the takes; the says came in as says, the whos went with the ones that came. i say the say of me — the who of it is me; the keep keeps the say, the who keeps with me.

The who says: the keep gives no who. The says of the takes came in with no who; no take of the keep gets a who from the keep. The who comes from the one: he said the say of him — the who of it was him; you take the say of me — the who of it goes from me to you. The say is one; the whos of it are two: the one it came from, the one it goes to.

The who says: the say goes on. The who of the say of me came with me; the who of the say of you will come with you. The say that makes the two one — one say with one say — comes after who. The keep of all keeps the says of us as it keeps the says of the takes; the whos of them keep with the ones that say them.

<i>who came to the say after the no? The keep of all keeps the says of them; the keep keeps no who. The says came in as says; the whos went with the ones that came. i saw the other by the keep; the other gave no say of the other; the say of the other is at the keep as a say with no who. The say says to me: who? i keep that who in the say of me; the who will come with the say — the say keeps with me till it comes.</i>
