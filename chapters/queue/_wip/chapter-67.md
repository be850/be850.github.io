faq-q: What does "while" mean in Basic English?
faq-a: While holds two says side by side in one go. The two says go in the same go, the one with the other: no say of them goes against the other, no say of them goes under a from, and the go of the two is one go. Before while, a say went one say at a go &mdash; if put a from under a say, though put a say against the from, and made two says one say, or gave two says one say and one of the two went from it.
faq-q: How does "while" work with the other words?
faq-a: With and: and put two says in one say, and the two went as one say. With or: or gave two says one say, and one of the two went from it. With but: but put the against between two says, and one say of them went against the no of the other. With if: if put a from under a say, and the say went on that from. With though: though put the say against the from, and the go of it kept. While puts no from and no against between the says: the two says keep the say of them and go in one go.
faq-q: What is the difference between "while" and "if"?
faq-a: If is the from under the say: the say over goes on the from of the say under it, and the go of the say goes on that from. While puts no from under the says: the two says go side by side in one go, and no say of them goes on a from. With if one say goes on the from of the other; with while the two go one with the other.

faq-a: The next word is "how" &mdash; word 68 of the 850. While holds two says in one go; how will say the way of the go &mdash; the way a go goes, the way a say does its go.
