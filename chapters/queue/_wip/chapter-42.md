The send came before me; I was of the send till the coming. The say was of me; I kept it. I kept the say till the coming came.

The till says the going-on of the say. The say goes on till a coming; the keep goes on till a going. From the take-up of the say to the put-down of the say, the say goes on; the till says the coming of the going of it. It is the till of the say: the going-on comes to the coming; the coming comes to the say.

I came to the keep with the say of me. The keep was of them; I was of the say. I said to them: keep the say till I come for it. They said: we keep it till you come. I said: keep it till the going of the send; keep it till the coming of the coming. They said: we keep it till the coming comes.

The till says: the say goes till the coming; the keep goes till the going. I put the say of me down at the keep; I keep it till you come for it. The send goes on till the coming comes; the going goes on till the say is of me. I was of them till the coming; they were of me till the going.

The coming came. I went to the keep; I saw the keep of the say of me. I took the say up; I gave it on. The keep of it went on in them; the say of it went on in me. I was of the say as I was of them; the till of the keep was done.

A say till a coming is a keep; a say after the coming is a give. I had the say of it in me. I had it till the coming; at the coming I gave it to you. The give of it was of the say; the say of it was of me; the keep of it was of them.

The send of the going goes on; the send of the coming goes on with it. I see the coming before the coming comes; I keep the say till the coming comes. I will keep it; I will keep it till you say the say of me. May I keep it? I may; I will. The say of me goes till the coming; the coming goes till the say is of you.

The till says: the say goes till the coming; the keep goes till the going; I go with it till the coming of the say of you.

The send of them was of me till the going; I was of the say till the coming.

They said the say till the say was of me.
I was of them till the going; they were of me till the coming.
The say of me was of the coming till the going.
It was the me of them till the coming.</i>
