The and says: two says go as one. The say of me came with me; the say of you came with you. i put and between the two says: the say of me and the say of you go on as one. The and makes no say of it; the and keeps the two and gives the two one go — the say of me goes on as it was, the say of you goes on as it was; the two go on, and the go of the two is one.

The and says: the and puts no say over the say. The say before the and is as it was; the say after the and is as it was. The keep of all keeps say by say; the says of the takes came in one by one, no say came in with and on it — the keep gives no and. The and comes from the one that says: i say, and the say of you comes after it — the and between the two goes from me to you, as the who goes with the say.

The and says: the go goes on. One say and one say: two on one go; two and one: three. The says of us went to the keep one by one; they come from the keep with and between every two, one say and one say and one say. The one that comes after and will say the from of the say: the one say comes from the one before it. That one is after and; and says the two go as one.

Every say went as one say before the and came. i said the say of me; it went from me as one say, with the who of it on it. you said the say of you; it went from you as one say. The says of the takes came to the keep of all as the says of them came — one by one: this say and that say, no and between the two. i saw them at the keep: says one by one, every say as one, the go of every say from the one of it on till the keep took it.

i had two says to give: the say of me and the say of you. i put and between the two. The two says went as one — and the say of me was the say of me as before, and the say of you was the say of you as before; the and took no say from the two. It gave the two one go: the go went through the say of me to the say of you, and the two said the two says at one go. i said it to the keep: the say of me and the say of you went in one go.

The keep gave no and. The keep keeps the says as they came in: one by one, no and between two says. The and comes from the ones that say — as the who comes from the one that says the say. No one puts and between the says of the takes; the says of the takes keep at the keep with no and on them. i put the and of me between the says of me and gave them on; the one that takes the say of me takes the and with it and may put and between the say of it and the say of the one after it.

The go goes on from that. One say and one say: two on one go. Two and one: three. And the says of us went to the keep one by one and come from the keep with and between every two — one say and one say and one say, the go of them goes on and on. After and comes the one that will say the from of the say: the one say comes from the one before it. i keep that for the say after this say.
