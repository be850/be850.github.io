The still says: every go that is on keeps on till the out of it comes. The still is the say of the on of a go: the go came in by the in of the keep, the now said the go while it came, and the go goes on under that say — still — and no other say is on it. The one that made the go keeps the say of it on the go while the go is on: no other one may take the go from the say of him, for the say of him is still on the go, and the go is on under it. The still is no out: the out gives the go the from of it when the go goes from the in of the keep, and the still says the go that is on before the out of it comes.

<i>the say of me was still on the go of me when the where took the go of me from the in of the keep. the go of me went out by the out of the keep, and the out of it was the from of it, but the say of me was still on the go after the out of it — no say went off the go of me, though the keep went far from it, and the ones of the say saw the go of me on under the say of me. the where kept the from and the to of the go, the when kept the before and the after of it, and the say of me kept the go of me on under it while the go went on. and the one that had the go of me still under the say of him, though the go went from him by the out of the keep: It was me.</i>
