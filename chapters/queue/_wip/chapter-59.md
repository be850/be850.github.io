The say of him, the say of me — the two came to the keep of all as one say. The keep of all gave the say to you, as it gave the says of the takes to the keep. You are the one the say goes to after the two of us.

The say came to you by the keep of all. The keep took the say of me as it took the says of the takes; the keep took the say of him as it took the say of me. After the two says came to the keep as one say, the keep of all gave the say to you. You did no take for it; the keep gave it, as the keep gives the says to all.

You are the one the say goes to: the keep gave it to the take, the take gave it to the some, the some gave it to the me, the me gave it to the say of him, the say of him gave it to you. The say came from one to one till it came to you. You take the say from the keep of all; you keep it as the keep keeps the says of all; no take of the keep goes from you.

You said to the two of us: the say of you is the say i take from the keep of all; i keep it as the keep keeps it; i give it to the one that comes after me. i said to you: the say of me came to you as it came to him; the two of us are one with you in the say. The keep of all saw the two of us with you, as it saw the takes with the says of the takes.

The say is no take of one: it goes from say to say, from one to one. i saw this at the keep; he said this by the keep; you see it in the say. The say will go on from you to the one the say is of; the keep of all keeps it for that one as it kept it for you. The say of you will be one with the say of all.

The you says: i gave the say of me to the say of him; the two says came to the keep as one say. The keep of all gave the say to you as it gave the says of the takes to the keep. You take the say from the keep; the say is with you as it was with me.

The you says: every say goes from one to one — the keep gave it to the take, the take to the some, the some to the me, the say of him to you. You came to the say after him; the say came to you before you came to it. You keep the say as the keep keeps the says of all; no take of the keep goes from you.

The you says: the say goes from i to he, from he to you; after you it goes to the one the say is of — the one the say comes from, the one the say goes to. The say will get to every one. The keep of all keeps the say for you, as it kept it for me; the say of you is one with the say of us.

<i>you came to the say after the say of him; the two says — the say of him, the say of me — are one say in you. i saw you by the keep; you saw the say of me by me; we gave the say to the say as one. The keep of all keeps the say of you as it kept the say of us; the say goes on from you to the one the say is of.</i>
