faq-q: What does "between" mean in Basic English?
faq-a: Between is the word of the middle space. It needs two things: one thing on this side, one thing on that side, and the between-thing in the middle of them. "I am between the send and the say" &mdash; the send is on one side, the say is on the other, and the hero stands in the middle. It is word 26 of the 850 &mdash; the eighth word in the line that began with "about". About gave the hero his subject; across gave him his road; after put his acts in their line; against gave the line a push the other way; among put him in the middle of the group; at took him to one point of it; before gave his acts their order in time; between gives him the space between two things.
faq-q: How does "between" work with the verbs?
faq-a: Between takes two things and puts a person, a thing or an act in the middle. With be: "I am between them" &mdash; the hero is the middle thing. With put: "They put the say between us" &mdash; the say stands in the middle of the two. With come: "The say came between: the send before it; the see after it." And with keep: "Keep it between us" &mdash; the thing stays in the middle; it does not go to a third person.
faq-q: What is the difference between "among" and "between"?
faq-a: Among (word 23) needs a group: one thing in the middle of many. Between needs exactly two: one thing between this one and that one. "I am among them" &mdash; the group is about the hero, four were five. "I am between the one and the four" &mdash; two ends, and the hero in the middle. Among is the word of the group; between is the word of the pair.
faq-q: What word comes after "between"?
faq-a: The next word is "by" &mdash; word 27 of the 850. Between is the word of the middle space between two things; by is the word of the doer and the near thing: "the send was sent by them" &mdash; by names the doer; "I was by the send" &mdash; by puts a person near a thing. The hero has his subject (19), his road (20), his order (21), his push back (22), his place in the group (23), his point (24), the order of his acts in time (25), and the middle space (26); the next chapter will name the doer and the near thing.
faq-q: Why is part of the chapter in italics?
faq-a: Italics are memory. The hero recovers his past one fragment per chapter, told only with the words the book has given him so far &mdash; the fragments form a second, hidden story under the main one. With among they put him among them; with at they kept him at it; with before the fragments gave the order of that night. With between the fragments show what stood in the middle: the say came between him and them; they said "You are the between. It is between you." The hidden story keeps saying the same thing from a new side: the middle was always him; it was me.
