faq-q: What does "as" mean in Basic English?
faq-a: As is the being of one thing in the place of another: "the say as the keep" &mdash; the say goes in the keep's place; "I see it as it is" &mdash; I see it the way it is. It is word 39 of the 850 &mdash; the next word in the line that began with "about". About gave the hero his subject; across gave him his road; after put his acts in their line; against gave the line a push the other way; among put him in the middle of the group; at took him to one point of it; before gave his acts their order in time; between gave him the middle space; by named the doer and kept the near thing near; down took him down the line to the low place; from said where the coming starts; in put him inside; off took a thing away; on put it back; over took it over; through took it through; to took the act to its end; under put the keep under the send; up took the say up from under; with keeps the say with him; as says the being of one in the place of the other.
faq-q: How does "as" work with the verbs?
faq-a: As goes with the verbs of seeing, keeping, going and taking. With see: "I see it as it is" &mdash; the seeing takes the thing the way it is. With keep: "I keep the say as the keep keeps the say" &mdash; the keeping goes the way the keep goes. With go: "I go as the say goes; the say goes as I go" &mdash; the two goings go together. With take: "I take it as I take the going" &mdash; the take puts one thing in the other's place.
faq-q: What is the difference between "with" and "as"?
faq-a: With and as are two sides of the keep. With says the keep side by side: "I take the say with me" &mdash; it goes where I go, next to me. As says the being in the place of: "the say is as the keep" &mdash; the say stands where the keep stands; it is the keep in the say's shape. In the chapter the say came with the hero, and then it stood as his keep: first with, then as.

faq-q: Why is part of the chapter in italics?
faq-a: Italics are memory. The hero recovers his past one fragment per chapter, told only with the words the book has given him so far &mdash; the fragments form a second, hidden story under the main one. With under the fragments said where the say was kept and who put it up. With with they said the going with. With as they say the being in the place of: they saw me as them; I went as them; I was as the one, and the one was as me. The say keeps with me. I go with it; it goes with me. The send comes; I see it come. I let it come; I see it as it is. The say goes with me as the going goes; I take it as I take the going.

The as says: I keep the say as the keep keeps the say; I go as the say goes; the say is as the keep. The send comes as the send comes; I see it as it is; I let it go as it goes. One say goes as one keep: the say as the keep, the keep as the say.

The send comes as the four came. I see the four: they come as one; they go as one. I take them as they come. The say goes with them; it goes as they go. I keep the say as the four keep it: one say, one keep, one going.

I see me as the send sees me. The send says: he keeps the say as the keep keeps it; he goes as the say goes. I let it say it. The say is as it is; I am as I am. I see the say as it is; the keep says it to me. I see me as the keep; the keep says me.

The send goes; the say keeps with me. It came up from under the four; it came as the four came. It is with me as the keep is with the going. I take it as I take the going: one going, one keep, one say.

The as says it: the say as the keep; the keep as the say. I go as the say goes; the say goes as I go. The send will come; I will see it as it is. The say will go as the going goes: with me, as me, as the keep goes with the going.
