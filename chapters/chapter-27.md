faq-q: What does "by" mean in Basic English?
faq-a: By is the word of the doer and the near thing. With a doer: "the send was made by them" &mdash; by names the one who made it. With a near thing: "I am by the send" &mdash; by puts a person or a thing near another thing. It is word 27 of the 850 &mdash; the next word in the line that began with "about". About gave the hero his subject; across gave him his road; after put his acts in their line; against gave the line a push the other way; among put him in the middle of the group; at took him to one point of it; before gave his acts their order in time; between gave him the middle space; by names the doer and keeps the near thing near.
faq-q: How does "by" work with the verbs?
faq-a: By takes a doer or a near thing and puts it close. With be: "I am by them" &mdash; the hero keeps near. With come: "They came by me; they came by the send" &mdash; the going goes past the near thing. With keep: "Keep me by you; I keep by you" &mdash; the thing stays near and does not go far. With make and say: "The send was made by them; the say was said by them" &mdash; by names the doer of the act.
faq-q: What is the difference between "between" and "by"?
faq-a: Between needs two things and a middle: "I am between the send and the say" &mdash; two ends, and the hero in the middle. By names a doer or puts a thing near a thing: "the say was made by me"; "I am by the say". Between is the word of the pair; by is the word of the doer and the near thing. Among is the word of the group, between is the word of the two, and by is the word at the side.
faq-q: What word comes after "by"?
faq-a: The next word is "down" &mdash; word 28 of the 850. By names the doer and the near thing: "the say was made by me"; "I am by the send". The hero has his subject (19), his road (20), his order (21), his push back (22), his place in the group (23), his point (24), the order of his acts in time (25), the middle space (26), and the doer and the near thing (27); the next chapter will take him down the line of his acts.
faq-q: Why is part of the chapter in italics?
faq-a: Italics are memory. The hero recovers his past one fragment per chapter, told only with the words the book has given him so far &mdash; the fragments form a second, hidden story under the main one. With before the fragments gave the order of that night; with between the fragments showed what stood in the middle. With by the fragments name the doer: the send was made by them; the say was made by me. They said: "You are the by. It was by you." The hidden story keeps saying the same thing from a new side: the doer was always him; it was me.
