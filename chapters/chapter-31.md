faq-a: Off is the word of the taking-away. faq-q: How does "off" work with the verbs?
faq-a: Off goes with the verbs of taking and going. With take: "I take it off" &mdash; it was on it, I take it away. With go: "the send goes off" &mdash; it is off, it is gone. With keep: "they keep it off me" &mdash; they keep it away from me. With put: "they put it off" &mdash; they take it away and keep it away. The word before the act says what goes; "off" says away.
