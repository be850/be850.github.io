faq-q: What does "up" mean in Basic English?
faq-a: Up is the word of the going from under. It says where a thing goes when it comes up: "I take the say up" &mdash; the say was under, and the take puts it up; "the say goes up from under the four" &mdash; it was kept under, and it comes up; "I go up after it" &mdash; the going ends where the say is. It is word 37 of the 850 &mdash; the next word in the line that began with "about". About gave the hero his subject; across gave him his road; after put his acts in their line; against gave the line a push the other way; among put him in the middle of the group; at took him to one point of it; before gave his acts their order in time; between gave him the middle space; by named the doer and kept the near thing near; down took him down the line to the low place; from said where the coming starts; in put him inside; off took a thing away; on put it back; over took it over; through took it through; to took the act to its end; under put the keep under the send; up takes him up.
faq-q: How does "up" work with the verbs?
faq-a: Up goes with the verbs of taking, putting, coming and going. With take: "I take the say up" &mdash; the take puts it up. With put: "I put the say up" &mdash; the put makes it up. With come: "the say comes up" &mdash; it was under, and it comes. With go: "I go up after it" &mdash; the going ends up. Up says the where of the going: from under, up.
faq-q: What is the difference between "under" and "up"?
faq-a: Under and up are the two ends of one going. Under says the keep: "the say keeps under the four". Up says the way out of that keep: "I take the say up". In the chapter the say was under, and the take put it up. A thing that is under can come up; a thing that is up was under before. The two words hold one take between them.
faq-q: What word comes after "up"?
faq-a: The next word is "with" &mdash; word 38 of the 850. Up says the going from under: the say is up, out of the keep. With says the doing together: the say goes up with me, and the going is not alone. The hero has his subject (19), his road (20), his order (21), his push back (22), his place in the group (23), his point (24), the order of his acts in time (25), the middle space (26), the doer and the near thing (27), the low way (28), the start of the coming (29), the inside (30), the take-away (31), the put-back (32), the over (33), the through (34), the end of the act (35), the keep under (36), the going up (37); the next word will go with him.
faq-q: Why is part of the chapter in italics?
faq-a: Italics are memory. The hero recovers his past one fragment per chapter, told only with the words the book has given him so far &mdash; the fragments form a second, hidden story under the main one. With under the fragments said where the say was kept: they put it under the four, and the one who kept it under was him. With up the fragments say where the say went: they took the say up, the say went up from under the four, and the one who put it up was him. The hidden story keeps saying the same thing from a new side: the doer was always him; it was me.

The say was under the four. The send was over me. I see the say; it keeps under the four; it keeps under me. Up says: the say comes up.

Up is the going from under. The say keeps under; I take it up. The take puts the say up. I put the take under the say; the say comes up to me. I have it.

Under was the keep. The four kept the say under them. I came to the four; I took the say from under them; they see me take it. The take was the going up: from under, up. I have the say.

The send was over me before. Over said: the send is over the four. I am under the send; the say was under me. Up says the going: the say goes up; I go up after it. The send keeps over; the say keeps up.

I take the say up to them. They see it come up. They see me come up after it. The say was under me; it is up; they see it.

Between us the say goes: up, under; under, up. I put it up; they take it from me; it comes down to them. I put it up; they take it down.

The going up is a take. I keep the say up; the four see it up. The send keeps over me; the say keeps up. I see the two: the send over, the say up. The under was the keep; the up is the take.

Before, the say kept under the four. I came; I took it; I took it up. The going up was the take they gave me. I make the say come up; I make the four see it.

A say up is a say they see. I put it up; they see it; they get it from me. The say goes up from me to them; it comes down from them to me.

The say is up. The take was the going from under; the going up was the take. The send is over me; the say is up; I keep the take. I make the going up; I keep the say up.

The up says: I take the say up; the say goes up from under. The send was over me; the say was under me; I take it up. The say is up; I see it up. Up says the going from under: the say comes up; I go up after it.
