faq-q: What is the difference between "he" and "him"?
faq-a: He is the doer; him is the one the say is about or the act comes to. You say he when he does: he came, he says, he keeps. You say him when the say is about him: the say of him, i saw him, the keep gave the say to him, the says went through him. He is the start of the say of him &mdash; the doer, the one the say comes from; him is the rest of the say &mdash; the one it is of, the one it goes to. In this book the two go together: he came to the keep; i saw him by the keep; we put the two says by the keep of all &mdash; he gave me the say of him, and i gave him the say of me.
