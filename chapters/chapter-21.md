faq-q: What does "after" mean in Basic English?
faq-a: After is the word of order in time: one thing goes, and the next thing comes after it. "I came after the send" &mdash; the send went, and my coming was the next thing. It is word 21 of the 850 &mdash; the third word of the list that is not a verb, after "about" and "across". About rounds a thing where it stands; across gives the road its far side; after puts the acts of the story in their line &mdash; this one goes, and that one comes after it. With about the hero got his subject; with across he got his road; with after he gets the order of his days.
faq-q: How does "after" work with the verbs?
faq-a: Two ways. Put it after a verb of moving and the move takes a road in time: "I go after them" &mdash; they go, and I go on their road, coming next; "they come after me" &mdash; I go before them, they come next. Put it with an act you say or keep, and the act comes a second time: "I say it after" &mdash; my saying comes when the first say is done. After may take a whole small sentence too: "After they sent the send, I saw it" &mdash; two acts, one line, the order inside it.
faq-q: What does "come after" mean?
faq-a: Three things, and the story uses them all. "They come after" &mdash; they arrive next; the coming took its place in the line. "They come after me" &mdash; they follow me; my going is before them on the same road. And a thing may "come after" a thing in time: the say came after the send &mdash; first the send, then the say. In the chapter all three are one: the hero goes, the others come after him, and what the send did comes after all.
faq-q: What word comes after "after"?
faq-a: The next word is "against" &mdash; word 22 of the 850. After puts acts in their line: this, then that. Against gives the line a push the other way: a thing may go after the road, or it may go against it. The hero has his subject (word 19), his road (word 20), his order (word 21); the next chapter gives him the push back &mdash; and the hidden story, which kept him after the send, will say what he stood against.

With after the order comes to light: the send went, and he came after the send &mdash; he is what came after his own send; the send was about him all the time.
