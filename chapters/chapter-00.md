## The promise

This book teaches you English with the 850 most useful words in the language —
the Basic English list made by C. K. Ogden in 1930. You start with one word.
Every new chapter adds exactly one word and uses only the words you already know.
No dictionary. No grammar drills. Just a story that grows as your vocabulary grows.

## The rule (the spiral)

- Chapter 1 uses one word: <em>come</em>.
- Chapter 2 adds <em>get</em>. Chapter 3 adds <em>give</em>. And so on, to word 850.
- You can read any chapter only after the words before it. The book is a staircase:
  every step is small, and every step holds the ones below it.
- A few helpers are free from day one: the pronouns (<em>I, you, he, she, it, we, they</em>),
  the numbers, and the little words <em>a</em> and <em>the</em>.

## The story

A man wakes up in a world where memory works like language: every word you own
is a thing you remember. He owns almost nothing. The first word that comes back
is <em>come</em>. The last word of the book is the first word again &mdash; and when
he says it, the circle closes.

## How to read

Read one chapter a day. Say every sentence out loud. When a chapter feels easy,
read it again &mdash; fast. The book is short at the start and long at the end,
because your head is bigger at the end than at the beginning.
