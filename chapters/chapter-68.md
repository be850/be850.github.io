faq-q: What does "how" mean in Basic English?
faq-a: How is the word that says the go of a say. A say goes, and the how says how the go went: it went up, or it went down; it went before a say, or it went after a say; it went with a say, or it went against a say. The how takes no say over the say and no say under the say &mdash; the who gave the ones of the say, the because gave the from under it, and the how gives the go of the say. faq-q: How does "how" work with the other words?
faq-a: With who: who gave the ones of a say &mdash; the one the say came from, the one the say goes to; how gives the go of the say. With because: because puts the from under the say, and the say after it goes from the say before it; how puts no from under a say &mdash; it goes with the go of the say and says the go of it. faq-q: What is the difference between "how" and "who"?
faq-a: Who says the ones of a say: the say came from one and goes to one. How says the go of the say: the say went up or down, before or after, with or against. A say may have a who and a how in one go &mdash; the who of it and the go of it; no one of the two takes the say of the other, and the keep keeps the say, the who of it and the how of it as it keeps every say.
